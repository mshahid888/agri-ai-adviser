# Wheat — General Agricultural Knowledge
## Crop
Wheat is a Rabi-season cereal crop.
## Rice-Wheat Rotation
Rice-wheat systems require attention to residue management, soil structure,
seedbed preparation, irrigation, and nutrient management.
## Residue Management
Rice residue should not be burned. Residue-management options can include
appropriate incorporation or suitable direct-seeding/zero-tillage equipment,
depending on local conditions and available machinery.
## Seedbed
Wheat generally benefits from a well-prepared, level seedbed with suitable
soil moisture and good seed-to-soil contact.
## Sowing
The appropriate sowing period depends on location, climate, variety,
cropping system, and local agricultural recommendations.
## Irrigation
Adequate soil moisture is important for establishment and subsequent crop
development. Irrigation requirements depend on rainfall, soil characteristics,
crop stage, and the irrigation system.
## Nutrient Management
Nutrient requirements should ideally be determined using soil-test information
and locally applicable agricultural recommendations. Nitrogen, phosphorus,
and potassium requirements vary according to soil fertility, previous crop,
yield target, and management system.
## Chemical Inputs
Pesticides, fungicides, herbicides, and other agricultural chemicals should
only be used according to the locally registered product label and applicable
regulations.
## Evidence Rule
Specific fertilizer rates, pesticide rates, varieties, disease-control
recommendations, and location-specific sowing recommendations require
verified local agricultural evidence rather than assumptions.
